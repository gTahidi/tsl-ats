# Referees

## What Is a Referee?
A **Referee** (sometimes called a reference) is someone who can vouch for a candidate’s experience. Referees are stored separately so that each candidate can have multiple references recorded with contact details.

## Adding a Referee to a Candidate
1. Open the candidate’s profile.
2. Navigate to the **Referees** tab and click **Add Referee**.
3. Fill in *Name*, *Relationship*, *Email/Phone*, and any notes.
4. Click **Save**. The referee now appears in the list.

## Viewing & Editing Referees
Select a referee from the list to see full details or make updates (contact info, notes). All edits are timestamped.

## Requesting Feedback
Once automation rules are configured (see *Automation*), you can trigger an email to the referee directly from the candidate’s page.
