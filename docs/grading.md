# Candidate Grading

The ATS lets you capture both **numeric scores** and **text ratings** at each process step. These roll up to an overall candidate rating visible on the Candidates list.

## Where Grades Come From
- **Process Step Rating** – entered manually by the interviewer for each step (e.g., 4/5 for Technical Interview).
- **Automation Rules** (future) – may update ratings based on assessments or test scores.

## Interpreting Ratings
| Icon / Color | Meaning |
|--------------|---------|
| 🟢 **Strong** | Highly recommended to proceed |
| 🟡 **Neutral** | Requires further evaluation |
| 🔴 **Reject** | Does not meet requirements |

(Your organization can customize categories in **Settings → Rating Scale**.)

## Tips for Consistent Grading
1. Define clear scoring criteria per step (e.g., 1–5 scale for technical skill, communication, culture fit).
2. Encourage interviewers to add short notes alongside scores.
3. Review aggregated ratings in the **Pipeline Overview** dashboard.
